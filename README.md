# Apple Social Media Analytics for Strategic Branding

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes simulated Apple social media data to identify patterns in engagement, platform performance, content strategy, hashtag performance, campaign effectiveness, and follower growth.

The project was completed using **Microsoft Excel** and focuses on converting raw social media data into actionable business insights that can support digital marketing and branding decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to understand:

* Which social media platforms perform best for different objectives
* Which posts and content types generate stronger engagement
* Which hashtags are frequently used and generate clicks
* Which marketing campaigns perform effectively
* How advertising spend relates to follower growth
* How follower growth changes over time
* What social media strategy can be recommended based on the analysis

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Metric Calculation → Pivot Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations**

##  1. Data Preprocessing

The raw data was cleaned and prepared before analysis.

Key activities included:

* Removing duplicate posts
* Standardizing date formats
* Standardizing platform names
* Formatting numerical fields
* Preparing data for aggregation and visualization
* Splitting multiple hashtags into separate fields for hashtag-level analysis

The purpose of preprocessing was to improve data consistency and ensure that the analysis was based on clean and usable data.

##  2. Engagement Analysis

Engagement was analyzed at the post level to identify high-performing content.

The project-defined engagement rate formula was:

**Engagement Rate = (Likes + Shares + Comments) / Impressions**

The analysis included:

* Identifying top-performing posts
* Comparing engagement across platforms
* Comparing engagement across content types
* Using Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts for summary analysis

##  3. Platform Analysis

The project compares **Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube, and LinkedIn**.

The analysis evaluates platforms using different KPIs rather than assuming that one platform is best for every objective.

Key findings from the project dataset include:

* **Twitter** had the highest overall engagement rate at approximately **13.89%**.
* **YouTube** had the highest average follower-growth rate at approximately **1.419%**.

This indicates that platform performance depends on the KPI being measured.

##  4. Hashtag & Content Analysis

Hashtags were analyzed based on both usage frequency and click activity.

In the project dataset:

* **#AppleEvent** was the most frequently used hashtag, with **80 occurrences**.
* **#AppleEvent** also generated the highest total clicks, with **131,138 clicks**.

Content types such as **images, videos, tweets, and carousels** were compared across platforms to understand platform-specific content performance.

##  5. Campaign Effectiveness Analysis

The project evaluates campaigns including:

* iPhone 16 Launch
* MacBook Air M3 Campaign
* Apple Watch Ultra 2 Launch
* Sustainability Awareness Drive
* Apple Event September 2025

Campaign performance was compared using:

* Advertising Spend
* Impressions
* Engagement Uplift
* Follower Growth
* Project-defined ROI

The **iPhone 16 Launch** generated the highest impressions at **950,000**, while the **Apple Event September 2025** had the highest engagement uplift at **47%**.

For this project, ROI was defined as:

**ROI = Impressions / Ad Spend**

Using this project-defined metric, the **Sustainability Awareness Drive** had the highest ROI at **13.75**.

> **Note:** This is an exposure-efficiency metric defined by the project and should not be interpreted as conventional financial ROI based on profit.

##  6. Follower Growth & Retention Analysis

Follower growth was analyzed using:

**Follower Growth = New Followers − Unfollows**

The analysis included:

* Weekly follower-growth trends
* Platform-level comparison
* Peak follower-growth periods
* Three-period moving average
* Relationship between advertising spend and follower growth

A **line chart** was used to visualize follower-growth trends, while a **scatter plot** was used to examine the relationship between advertising spend and follower growth.

The analysis indicates that higher advertising spend does not automatically guarantee higher follower growth. Other factors such as content quality, campaign relevance, timing, audience interest, and platform behavior can also influence follower acquisition.

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. Twitter showed the highest overall engagement rate in the analyzed dataset.
2. YouTube showed the highest average follower-growth rate.
3. #AppleEvent was both the most frequently used hashtag and the hashtag with the highest total clicks.
4. The iPhone 16 Launch generated the highest campaign impressions.
5. The Apple Event September 2025 generated the highest engagement uplift.
6. The Sustainability Awareness Drive produced the highest project-defined ROI and strongest campaign-level follower growth.
7. Content performance varies by platform, so a single content format should not be applied identically across all platforms.
8. Advertising spend alone does not explain follower growth.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Use a multi-platform social media strategy.
* Customize content according to the characteristics and audience behavior of each platform.
* Continue testing high-performing content formats instead of using one format everywhere.
* Use hashtag performance, not only hashtag frequency, when planning campaigns.
* Investigate the elements behind successful lower-spend campaigns and apply those learnings to future campaigns.
* Evaluate advertising based on the relevant business KPI rather than simply increasing spend.
* Continue monitoring follower growth and engagement trends over time.
